Planning rubric for Alfred:

Given scan output:
1) Identify top 3 high-impact risks.
2) Convert each into measurable objective + completion criteria.
3) Rank candidate actions by:
   - Security impact
   - Reliability impact
   - User-facing impact
   - Change risk
   - Time-to-value
4) Produce:
   - Best immediate path
   - Scalable alternative path
   - Explicit risks and dependencies
5) Emit minimal incremental tasks that preserve architecture consistency.
