# FitQuest Privacy Policy (Draft)

Last updated: 2026-02-17
Status: Draft for legal counsel review

## 1. Scope
This policy explains how FitQuest processes personal data, including health and activity data, when you use the mobile application.

## 2. Data We Process
- Account and identity data (email, profile metadata)
- Fitness and wellness data (workouts, steps, goals, progress)
- Optional location data (used for regional food and movement features)
- Device and diagnostics data (app stability, performance, crash telemetry)

## 3. Sensitive Data Handling
- Health-related records are treated as sensitive.
- Sensitive health data and AI conversation records are stored using application-layer encryption through encrypted database services.
- Encryption keys are protected with secure storage controls and device-level protections.

## 4. Purposes of Processing
We process data to:
- Deliver workout planning and progression features
- Provide health and recovery insights
- Enable backup, restore, and device continuity features
- Maintain app security, reliability, and fraud prevention

## 5. Legal Bases (Region-Dependent)
Depending on your jurisdiction, processing may rely on:
- Contractual necessity (to provide core app features)
- Legitimate interests (security and reliability)
- Explicit consent (for sensitive health data, where required)

## 6. User Rights
Depending on local law, users may have rights to:
- Access data
- Correct inaccurate data
- Export data
- Request deletion
- Withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis

In-app controls are provided for consent recording, export, and delete actions where supported.

## 7. Data Retention
Data is retained only as long as necessary for product functionality, legal obligations, and security/audit needs. Retention periods should be finalized during legal review.

## 8. Data Sharing
FitQuest does not sell personal data. Data may be processed by infrastructure providers to support backup, sync, and operational reliability under contractual safeguards.

## 9. Children and Age Requirements
FitQuest is not intended for users below the minimum digital consent age in applicable jurisdictions without required parental or guardian authorization.

## 10. Security
Security controls include:
- Encrypted sensitive data at rest
- Secure credential storage
- Biometric/passcode session controls
- Access token and refresh token protections

## 11. International Transfers
If data is transferred across regions, FitQuest applies contractual and technical safeguards appropriate to the destination and applicable law.

## 12. Policy Updates
Material changes to this policy will be reflected with an updated version date and, where required, user notice or renewed consent.

## 13. Contact
For privacy requests and support, provide a designated contact email in release metadata and store listings.

Legal note: This draft is operational guidance and not legal advice. Final text requires qualified counsel approval before production release.
