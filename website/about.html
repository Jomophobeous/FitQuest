Our Story

Born in Lesotho,
Built for the World

FitQuest started with a simple question: Why isn't there a fitness app that trains your body AND your mind — one that works completely offline and never sells your data?

"I grew up in Lesotho — the Mountain Kingdom. A place of breathtaking landscapes but limited access to the resources that fuel personal growth in other parts of the world. Gyms, personal trainers, health tech, cognitive science tools — they were always somewhere else, for someone else. FitQuest was born from that gap. It's the tool I needed but couldn't find. An entire ecosystem — workouts, health tracking, reading, learning — all in one app, working offline, encrypted, and free from the surveillance economy."

— The Creator
What We Believe

Values That Drive Us

🏔️

Accessibility

World-class fitness technology shouldn't require a gym membership or a fast internet connection. FitQuest works anywhere — from Maseru to Manhattan, offline and on.

🔐

Privacy by Design

We chose to have no servers. Not because it's easier — it's harder. Because your health data belongs to you and only you. AES-256-GCM encryption seals that promise.

🧠

Holistic Growth

Physical fitness is only half the equation. FitMind — our cognitive fitness module — trains your mind with spaced repetition, reading analytics, and AI-powered learning.

🧬

Science-Backed

Every algorithm is grounded in published research: Mifflin-St Jeor for BMR, Karvonen for heart rate zones, Brzycki for 1RM, SM-2 for spaced repetition, Navy method for body fat.

🇱🇸

Proudly African

Built in the Kingdom of Lesotho with pride. We believe innovation can come from anywhere — and that Africa has ideas the world needs. This is just the beginning.

⚡

Evolution, Not Perfection

We don't chase perfection — we chase progress. FitQuest evolves with every update, every user's feedback, and every lesson learned. The journey is the product.

“

FitQuest isn't just an app I built — it's the app I use. Every morning. Every workout. Every page I read. I am my own first user, my own harshest critic, and my own biggest believer. If it's not good enough for me, it's not shipping.

— The Creator of FitQuest
Under the Hood

Technology Stack

Engineered for performance, security, and reliability on every device.

Frontend

  • → React Native 0.83 + Expo SDK 55
  • → Expo Router v6 (file-based navigation)
  • → New Architecture (Fabric + TurboModules)
  • → Reanimated 3 for 60fps animations

Data & Security

  • → SQLite (on-device, 15-version schema)
  • → AES-256-GCM application-layer encryption
  • → PBKDF2 key derivation (1000 iterations)
  • → Biometric auth with session management

AI & Analytics

  • → On-device AI (template + context injection)
  • → Statistical anomaly detection (Z-score, IQR)
  • → Sensor fusion (accelerometer + gyroscope)
  • → SM-2 spaced repetition for flashcards

Health & Fitness

  • → 1,300+ exercises across 6 categories
  • → Fatigue-aware workout generation
  • → Sleep analysis & recovery scoring
  • → 15-language internationalization
