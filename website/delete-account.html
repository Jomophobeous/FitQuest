Data Management

Delete Your Account & Data

Your data, your decision. Here's how to delete everything.

🔑 Key Fact

FitQuest stores ALL data exclusively on your device. We have no servers and no cloud copies of your data. Deletion is immediate and permanent — there is no "undo" and no backup we can restore from.

Three Ways to Delete Your Data

📱

In-App Deletion

Full control, selective or complete.

  1. Open FitQuest
  2. Go to Profile tab
  3. Scroll to Settings
  4. Tap "Delete Account"
  5. Confirm with biometric/passcode
  6. All data is permanently erased
🗑️

Uninstall App

Quickest way — removes everything.

  1. Long-press the FitQuest icon
  2. Tap "Uninstall"
  3. Confirm

All app data, databases, cached files, encryption keys, and preferences are removed when the app is uninstalled.

📧

Email Request

For help or confirmation.

Email fitquestsupp0rt@gmail.com with subject "Data Deletion Request".

Since we have no server-side data, we'll respond with confirmation that no data exists on our end and guide you through on-device deletion if needed.

⚠️ Subscription Note

Deleting your data or uninstalling the app does NOT cancel your subscription. You must cancel your subscription separately through Google Play or the App Store to stop future charges.

Android: Google Play → Menu → Subscriptions → FitQuest → Cancel Subscription

iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → FitQuest → Cancel Subscription

What About Server-Side Data?

There is none. FitQuest has no backend servers, no cloud databases, and no data warehouses. The only external service is RevenueCat, which stores an anonymous subscriber token (no personal information) for subscription verification.

This means:

  • There is nothing for us to look up or delete on our end.
  • We cannot recover your data after deletion — there are no backups.
  • When data is deleted from your device, it is gone permanently.

🚨 This is Irreversible

Once you delete your data or uninstall FitQuest, all data is permanently gone. We have no backup, no cloud copy, and no way to recover it. This includes your workout history, health records, reading progress, flashcards, streaks, XP, and all settings. Make sure this is what you want before proceeding.
