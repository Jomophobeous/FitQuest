Features

Everything You Need to Transform

A complete fitness ecosystem built around your goals, your schedule, and your privacy.

🧠

AI-Adaptive Workouts

Intelligent workout generation that adapts to your fatigue levels, goals, available equipment, and real-time progression. Every session is uniquely built for you.

❤️

Health Monitoring

Track heart rate, steps, sleep quality, and recovery scores. Statistical anomaly detection alerts you to unusual patterns. Data sampled every minute for maximum accuracy.

📚

FitMind — Cognitive Fitness

A built-in document library with spaced repetition flashcards and dual AI assistants. Train your mind alongside your body for complete transformation.

🔒

Privacy-First Architecture

All data stays on your device. Health records encrypted with AES-256-GCM — the same standard used by banks and governments. Biometric lock. Zero cloud dependency.

🏃

Move & Track

Step counting via sensor fusion, jog routes with GPS mapping, calorie estimation using Mifflin-St Jeor equations, and active minute goals — all tracked automatically.

🤖

Dual AI Assistants

Coach for workout guidance, form cues, and motivation. Professor for knowledge, comprehension, and Socratic learning prompts. Two AI personalities, one mission.
