Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026 · Effective: March 2026

🔒 Privacy at a Glance

FitQuest is a fully offline app. Your data never leaves your device. Health data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. We have no servers, no accounts, and no cloud storage. We literally cannot see your data.

1. Who We Are

FitQuest is developed and published by fitquest.dev, domiciled in the Kingdom of Lesotho. For privacy inquiries, contact us at fitquestsupp0rt@gmail.com.

2. Architecture — Why We're Different

FitQuest is designed as a fully client-side application with no backend servers. This architectural decision means:

  • All workout data, health metrics, and profile information are stored exclusively on your device using SQLite.
  • There is no user account system — no email, no password, no cloud identity.
  • The app functions 100% offline. An internet connection is only needed for initial download and subscription verification.
  • We have no ability to access, read, or extract your data remotely.

3b. Subscription Verification

When you subscribe, FitQuest verifies your subscription status through RevenueCat:

  • An anonymous subscriber ID is exchanged with RevenueCat.
  • An anonymous app instance ID is generated.
  • No name, email, or payment details are transmitted by the app.
  • All payment processing is handled by the Google Play Store or Apple App Store — we never see, process, or store your credit card or billing information.

3c. Health Connect / HealthKit (Optional)

If you grant permission, FitQuest reads health data from Google Health Connect (Android) or Apple HealthKit (iOS):

  • Data types: step count, heart rate, sleep sessions, active energy.
  • This data is read into the app only and encrypted on-device.
  • FitQuest never writes back to Health Connect / HealthKit without explicit user action.
  • You can revoke access at any time in your device settings.

4. Encryption & Security

All sensitive health data is encrypted using AES-256-GCM (Advanced Encryption Standard with Galois/Counter Mode):

  • Key derivation: Master encryption key generated on first launch and stored in device secure enclave (Keychain on iOS, Keystore on Android).
  • Scope: Heart rate, sleep, AI conversations, health alerts, notes, and anomaly data are all encrypted at rest.
  • Authentication: GCM mode provides both confidentiality and data integrity verification.
  • Access: Biometric authentication (fingerprint/face) or PBKDF2-hardened passcode required to unlock encrypted data.
  • Emergency wipe: After 15 consecutive failed authentication attempts, all encrypted data is securely wiped.

5. Background Health Collection

When enabled, FitQuest collects health data in the background at 1-minute intervals:

  • Step count, heart rate (if available from sensors), and active minutes.
  • Statistical anomaly detection runs every 30 minutes to check for unusual health patterns.
  • Daily health summaries are auto-generated and stored encrypted.
  • Background collection can be disabled at any time in app settings.
  • All collected data remains 100% on-device and encrypted.

6. AI Assistants

FitQuest includes two AI assistants (Coach & Professor):

  • When online, AI responses are generated via third-party LLM providers (Groq and OpenRouter as fallback). Only your typed text prompts are sent — no health data, workout records, or personal information is transmitted.
  • When offline, template-based response generation provides guidance without any external calls.
  • All conversation history is encrypted with AES-256-GCM and stored locally on your device.
  • The AI does not learn from or retain your data between sessions beyond what you explicitly see in your conversation history.
  • You can review Groq's privacy policy at groq.com and OpenRouter's at openrouter.ai.

8. Children's Privacy

FitQuest is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Since all data is on-device and we have no server-side data collection, this risk is inherently mitigated by architecture.

9. Your Rights

Under the Lesotho Data Protection Act 2011, South Africa's POPIA, the EU GDPR, and CCPA, you have the right to:

  • Access: View all your data directly in the app (it's on your device).
  • Delete: Delete all data via Settings → Delete Account, or uninstall the app. See our Data Deletion page for instructions.
  • Portability: Your data lives on your device — you already have full access.
  • Object: You can disable any data collection feature (background health, Health Connect, etc.).
  • Restrict processing: All processing is local — disable features you don't want.

Since we have no servers and no centralized data store, most data rights are self-service: your device, your data, your control.

10. POPIA Compliance (South Africa)

For users in South Africa, FitQuest's architecture inherently satisfies POPIA's core principles:

  • Minimality: We collect only fitness-relevant data needed for the app's functionality.
  • Purpose limitation: Data is used exclusively for your personal fitness tracking.
  • Safeguards: AES-256-GCM encryption, biometric authentication, PBKDF2 key derivation.
  • No transborder transfer: Data never leaves your device, so no cross-border concerns arise.

11. Data Retention

  • Data persists on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • We have no server-side data, so there is nothing to retain on our end.
  • Subscription verification tokens are anonymous and managed by RevenueCat.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy to reflect product or regulatory changes. The "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly. Material changes will be communicated via an in-app notice.

13. Governing Law

This privacy policy is governed by the laws of the Kingdom of Lesotho, including the Data Protection Act 2011. Disputes shall be resolved in the courts of Maseru, Lesotho.
