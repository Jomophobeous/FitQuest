FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

General

What is FitQuest? +

FitQuest is an AI-powered fitness app that combines adaptive workouts, health monitoring, and cognitive fitness tools into one platform. It works 100% offline, encrypts all sensitive data with AES-256-GCM, and is available in 15 languages. Built in the Kingdom of Lesotho.

Does FitQuest require an internet connection? +

No. FitQuest is fully offline. The only time internet is needed is for (1) downloading the app from the Play Store / App Store and (2) verifying your subscription status. All workouts, health tracking, and AI features work without connectivity.

What devices does FitQuest support? +

FitQuest is available on Android (requires Android 8.0+ / API level 26+). iOS support is in development. The app is optimized for budget-to-mid-range devices and has been specifically tested on Samsung Galaxy A-series phones.

Subscription & Billing

How does the 14-day free trial work? +

When you first install FitQuest, you get 14 days of full premium access — no credit card required. You can use every feature: AI workouts, health monitoring, FitMind, Craft My Body, and more. After 14 days, you choose to subscribe or the premium features lock.

What are the subscription plans? +

Monthly: M44/month — charged monthly, cancel anytime.
Annual: M35/month billed at M420/year — save M108 (~20% off). Both plans include full access to all features. No ads, no hidden fees.

How do I cancel my subscription? +

Since all billing is handled by Google Play or the App Store, cancel through your device:
Android: Google Play → Menu → Subscriptions → FitQuest → Cancel.
iOS: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → FitQuest → Cancel.
Your premium access continues until the end of the current billing period. Your data is never deleted when you cancel.

How do I get a refund? +

Refunds are processed by Google Play or Apple, not by FitQuest. Visit the Google Play or Apple support pages for their refund policies. We recommend cancelling your subscription first and then requesting a refund through the platform.

Privacy & Security

Is my data safe? +

Yes. FitQuest has no servers — all data stays on your device. Sensitive health data (heart rate, sleep, AI conversations) is encrypted with AES-256-GCM. Access requires biometric authentication (fingerprint/face) or a PBKDF2-hardened passcode. After 15 failed auth attempts, encrypted data is automatically wiped.

Does FitQuest sell my data? +

No. We don't sell, share, or transmit any user data. We literally cannot — we have no servers to receive it. There are no analytics SDKs, no ad trackers, and no data brokers. Your data is yours and only yours.

How do I delete my data? +

Three options: (1) Use the in-app Delete Account feature in Settings. (2) Uninstall the app — this removes all data. (3) For selective deletion, manage data through the app's settings screens. See our Data Deletion page for detailed instructions.

Troubleshooting

The app is crashing or not loading +
  1. Force-close the app and reopen it.
  2. Ensure you're running the latest version from Google Play.
  3. Clear the app cache: Settings → Apps → FitQuest → Storage → Clear Cache.
  4. Restart your device.
  5. If the issue persists, email us at fitquestsupp0rt@gmail.com with your device model and Android version.
Steps aren't counting correctly +
  1. Ensure FitQuest has permission to access motion sensors (Settings → Apps → FitQuest → Permissions).
  2. If using Health Connect, make sure FitQuest has step count read permission.
  3. Check that battery optimization isn't killing background processes: Settings → Battery → FitQuest → Unrestricted.
  4. FitQuest uses sensor fusion (accelerometer + gyroscope) — accuracy may vary by device hardware.
My subscription isn't being recognized +
  1. Ensure you have an active internet connection (needed for subscription verification only).
  2. Check your subscription status in Google Play → Subscriptions.
  3. Force-close and reopen FitQuest.
  4. If you subscribed on a different Google account, ensure the correct account is active on your device.
  5. Contact us at fitquestsupp0rt@gmail.com if the issue continues.
